# VacAsia content and asset credits

The destination summaries are original English and Vietnamese copy, informed by the tourism sources below. Sources were checked on 7 October 2026. Links on each destination lead to the relevant tourism organization or visitor information site.

Daily budgets are rough example estimates in USD per person, intended for comparing destinations. They exclude flights and are not quotations. Activity-pass prices are demonstration prices for the app's simulated booking flow. Ratings are illustrative editorial scores, not customer reviews. Suggested trip lengths and recommended months are editorial planning suggestions rather than guarantees, availability information, or a weather forecast. Map coordinates indicate the general destination, not a specific meeting point.
